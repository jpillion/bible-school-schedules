Privacy Policy

Bible School Schedules (CBSSA) · Last updated 12 August 2026

This app collects no personal information of any kind. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and it makes no network requests.

What the app stores

Everything the app needs is included in the app itself when you install it:

  • The Bible School schedule. Bundled with the app. It is not downloaded and not fetched from a server.
  • Your two settings — whether reminders are switched on, and how many minutes' notice you want. These are saved on your own device only.

Nothing in that list is transmitted anywhere, because the app does not contain any code that sends data off the device.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal detail.
  • It does not create an account or require a sign-in.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, or any advertising or tracking service.
  • It does not request your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • It does not share or sell any data, because it holds none to share.

Notifications

If you switch reminders on, the app asks your phone's operating system for permission to show notifications. The reminders are then scheduled by the phone itself, from the schedule already stored on it. No reminder is sent from a server, and no push notification service is used — which is why reminders keep working when there is no signal at the camp.

Permissions, and why each one is needed

  • Notifications — to show the event reminders you asked for.
  • Exact alarms (Android) — so that "10 minutes before" actually means ten minutes before, rather than whenever the system next gets round to it.
  • Run at startup (Android) — scheduled alarms are discarded when a phone restarts; this permission lets the app quietly set them again.

The Home Screen web version

The web version at this site behaves the same way. It stores the schedule in your browser's cache so it works offline, sets no cookies, and sends nothing anywhere. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages; as with any web host, GitHub may keep its own server logs of requests, which is outside our control and covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app is a personal schedule and reminder tool. It is not directed at children, and since it collects no information at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Given that the app collects nothing, material change is unlikely.
